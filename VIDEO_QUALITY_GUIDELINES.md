# VIDEO QUALITY GUIDELINES (Reusable for Any Topic)

## Core Rule
> **Build the video around ONE visual metaphor that fits the topic.**
> Every scene shows that metaphor changing. The picture must explain what the voiceover says.

---

## 1. Objects & Anatomy
- **Recognizable Real-World Things**: Every object must be a recognizable real-world thing built from many small, tangible parts—never a generic plain box or slab.
- **Teardown Sub-Parts**: Break each object into labeled sub-components, just like a mechanical product teardown or x-ray inspection.
- **Always Moving**: Every scene needs moving parts (gears turning, pieces sliding apart, components falling/assembling, gauges sweeping, fluid/particle flow). **Nothing sits still.**
- **Before / After on the Same Object**: Show the problem state on the object, then visibly show the fix being applied to that exact same object.
- **Real Scale Cues**: Include scale cues (tiny stylized characters, hands, tools, or props) to ground physical dimension.

---

## 2. Camera & Pacing
- **Cut Every 2–3 Seconds**: Aim for 10+ distinct scenes/cuts in ~29 seconds.
- **Continuous Camera Movement**: Never hold one static view for more than 3 seconds. Constantly drift:
  - Wide shots
  - Push-ins on a specific component or slab
  - Pull-back reveals
  - Slow 3D orbits (yaw/pitch drifts)
- **First 2 Seconds Hook**: Shock visual or bold statement within the first 2 seconds.
- **Clear CTA Ending**: Conclude with a punchy call to action (e.g., *"Book a call"* / `@mwd1504`).

---

## 3. Look & Materiality
- **Lit 3D Geometry**: Real material properties (specular glass, metallic chassis, glowing neon conductors, and depth shading).
- **Soft Shadows**: Multi-tier diffuse contact shadows on the floor/platform plane to ground objects in 3D space.
- **Color Palette**: Dark background (deep obsidian/slate `#0A0C12`) with **one warm accent color** (amber/gold `#FFB020`, `#FFD700`) paired with purposeful status colors (cyan, emerald, crimson). No flat-fill shapes.

---

## 4. Typography & Captions
- **3–5 Words at a Time**: Large typography placed bottom-center.
- **Active Spoken Word Highlighted**: The currently spoken word must be highlighted in vibrant gold/amber with contrast drop shadow.
- **No Paragraph Boxes**: Remove heavy opaque text containers. Let text float cleanly with shadow contrast over the scene.
- **Precision Callouts**: Short HUD leader-lines pointing directly to specific sub-parts of the object.
- **Big Visual Numbers**: Never display critical metrics as small tags. Make numbers the visual centerpiece (e.g. `68%`, `3.8s ➔ 0.8s`) as animated counters or sweeping radial gauges.

---

## 5. Proof Beat
- **Include One Verified Proof Beat**: A real screenshot, platform audit (e.g., Google PageSpeed 99/100, Core Web Vitals), or a cited industry statistic with its source (e.g., *Think With Google*).

---

## 6. Pre-Delivery Audit Checklist ("BEFORE DELIVERING, CHECK")
Before approving or delivering any video, the auditor must verify:
1. [ ] **Angle & Movement**: Does each scene have a different angle, with parts moving?
2. [ ] **Clarity of Objects**: Can you tell what each object is without reading its label?
3. [ ] **No Static Holds**: Is there anything on screen for more than 3 seconds without changing?
4. [ ] **Pacing**: Does the video feature 10+ scene cuts across ~29s (cuts every 2–3s)?
5. [ ] **Metaphor Consistency**: Is the single central metaphor carried through from problem to resolution?
6. [ ] **Proof Beat & CTA**: Are both a verified proof stat and a clear CTA present?
