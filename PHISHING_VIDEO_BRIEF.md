6. Data (cookies, passwords, 2FA codes) streams out of the phone as small labeled objects flying to a hacker laptop.
7. Proof: a real phishing-report chart with the Verizon DBIR 91% stat, text inside the card edges.
8. Defense: a shield clamps over the link and the SMS turns green.
